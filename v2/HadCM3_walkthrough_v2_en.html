How HadCM3 computes, step by step

v2Each component below is drawn as a network of routines, the variables they pass between them, and every parameter they use. Version 1 keeps the simpler linear charts.

The main program, U_MODEL, works in coupled days. It runs the atmosphere through a whole day in 48 steps of 30 minutes, hands the day’s mean fluxes to the ocean, and runs the ocean through the same day in 24 steps of 1 hour. Then it hands SST and sea ice back and starts the next day. Inside every atmosphere step the order is fixed: dynamics first, then physics.

Numbers come from tdaah, a standard pre-industrial job among the shared BRIDGE UMUI jobs on bc5: 280 ppm CO₂, preind2 ancillaries, MOSES 2.1 with TRIFFID, a 360-day calendar starting 1 December 1850. Call order and line numbers are from the UM vn4.5 source decks.

Components

What each component computes

Each tab draws one component as a network. Numbered boxes are its routines in call order, joined by the thick line; plain boxes are the variables they hand to each other; coloured rounded boxes enter from or leave to another component, in that component’s colour; dashed boxes list the parameters each routine uses, with their tdaah values. Point at any box to light up its connections, click it to pin it and read what it reads and writes, and drag the background to pan. Dashed routines run only on some steps; grey ones are switched off in this job.
