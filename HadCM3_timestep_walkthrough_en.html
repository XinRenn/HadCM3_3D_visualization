How HadCM3 computes, step by step

The main program, U_MODEL, works in coupled days. It runs the atmosphere through a whole day in 48 steps of 30 minutes, hands the day’s mean fluxes to the ocean, and runs the ocean through the same day in 24 steps of 1 hour. Then it hands SST and sea ice back and starts the next day. Inside every atmosphere step the order is fixed: dynamics first, then physics.

Numbers come from tdaah, a standard pre-industrial job among the shared BRIDGE UMUI jobs on bc5: 280 ppm CO₂, preind2 ancillaries, MOSES 2.1 with TRIFFID, a 360-day calendar starting 1 December 1850. Call order and line numbers are from the UM vn4.5 source decks.
